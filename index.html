THE COLLECTION

A Civilization of Knowledge

Indian Knowledge Systems represent centuries of accumulated knowledge across science, mathematics, medicine, philosophy, architecture, language, arts and everyday practices.

SCIENCE & INQUIRY

Mathematical formulations, celestial astronomy, metallurgy, and botanical medicine grounded in empirical traditions.

PHILOSOPHY & MIND

Epistemological systems, logic (Nyaya), linguistics (Vyakarana), and mind-body holistic disciplines.

CULTURE & CRAFTSMANSHIP

Temple sacred geometry, Shilpa Shastra, metallurgy, performing arts, and sustainable living practices.

CHRONOLOGY

A Journey Through Time

Explore key milestones in the development of Indian Knowledge Systems across centuries.

c. 1500–500 BCE
Ancient Traditions

Vedic Traditions & Early Foundations

Oral transmission, calendar astronomy, phonetic linguistics (Shiksha), and geometric altar planning in the Sulba Sutras.

c. 500 BCE – 300 CE
Classical Foundations

Classical Foundations & Arithmetic

Panini’s generative grammar, Charaka and Sushruta encyclopedias, and early birch-bark arithmetic including zero notation.

c. 476–1200 CE
Astronomy & Mathematics

Aryabhata & Observational Science

Aryabhatiya introduces planetary orbits, trigonometric sine tables, and calculating eclipses through shadow projection.

Today
Centers of Learning to Modern Era

Knowledge Meets Technology

From university monastic centers like Nalanda to modern digital archives, traditional manuscripts are preserved for future generations.

MUSEUM GALLERIES

Explore the Galleries

Step inside six dedicated museum rooms documenting core intellectual traditions.

GALLERY 01 · MATHEMATICS

Numbers, Geometry & Calculation

Bakhshali Manuscript · Sulba Sutras · Brahmagupta

Explore decimal notation, positional zero, algebraic equations, and geometric transformation traditions pioneered by Indian scholars.

GALLERY 02 · ASTRONOMY

Observing the Cosmos

Jyotisha · Planetary Motion · Observatories

Historical traditions of planetary calculations, lunar mansions (Nakshatras), celestial armillary globes, and monumental masonry sundials.

GALLERY 03 · MEDICINE

Ayurveda & Medical Knowledge

Charaka Samhita · Sushruta Tradition · Herbology

Examine traditional approaches to health, Tridosha equilibrium, natural plant-based pharmacology, and historical surgical procedures.

GALLERY 04 · ARCHITECTURE

Sacred Geometry & Architecture

Kailasa Temple, Ellora · Vastu Vidya · Monolithic Carving

Study spatial planning, rock excavation from top to bottom, structural acoustics, and sacred proportional mandalas in Indian architecture.

GALLERY 05 · METALLURGY

Mastery of Metals

Iron Pillar of Delhi · Wootz Steel · Lost-Wax Bronze

Discover India's metallurgical marvels, including rust-resistant forge-welded wrought iron and crucible crucible Damascus steel production.

GALLERY 06 · ARTS & CULTURE

Stories, Language & Expression

Belur Sculptures · Natyashastra · Classical Epics

Engage with aesthetic theory (Rasa), master soapstone carving, Paninian linguistic precision, and Indian classical performing arts.

MUSEUM MISSION

Why a Digital Museum?

A digital museum allows historical knowledge, manuscripts, artifacts, images and stories to be documented and made accessible to learners beyond the physical walls of any single museum.

PRESERVE

Digitally document knowledge, artifacts and cultural resources for future generations, preventing the loss of fragile manuscripts.

DOCUMENT

Collate historical records, commentaries, metadata and open-access images into an organized educational repository.

EXPLORE

Allow students and learners to discover India's heritage without requiring physical access to museums or distant archives.
